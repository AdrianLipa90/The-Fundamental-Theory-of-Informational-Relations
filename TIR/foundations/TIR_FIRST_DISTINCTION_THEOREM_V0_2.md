## 1. Primitive relation

The minimum admitted non-empty TIR content is

\[
\boxed{\mathcal R_1=(a\xleftrightarrow{R}b).}
\]

The endpoints are roles of the relation, not independent primitive point-substances.

## 2. Primitive distinction operator

Orientation reversal is

\[
\boxed{
J:(a,b;R)\mapsto(b,a;R^{-1}),
\qquad
J^2=\mathrm{id}.
}
\]

For a non-self-collapsed relation the two orientation roles are distinct. Define

\[
N=[a\to b],
\qquad
S=[b\to a].
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal O_J=\{N,S\}.}
\]

### Theorem 1 — Primitive binary orbit

A nontrivial dyadic relation with orientation reversal has exactly two orientation roles in the orbit generated by \(J\).

## 3. Unique symmetric normalized share

Attach normalized nonnegative shares

\[
w_N,w_S\ge0,
\qquad
w_N+w_S=1.
\]

At exchange balance,

\[
(w_N,w_S)=(w_S,w_N),
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{w_N=w_S=\frac12.}
\]

### Theorem 2 — Unique exchange-invariant share

The unique normalized nonnegative assignment invariant under exchange of the two orientation roles is

\[
\boxed{\left(\frac12,\frac12\right)}.
\]

## 4. Half-seam coordinate

Let

\[
u=w_S,
\qquad
w_N=1-u.
\]

Then

\[
J(u)=1-u,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Fix}(J)=\left\{\frac12\right\}.}
\]

\[
H_2(u)=-(1-u)\ln(1-u)-u\ln u,

\[
\boxed{

## 6. Sphere before quantum

The pole pair defines an oriented axis. The complete normalized orientation orbit is

\[
\boxed{SO(3)/SO(2)\cong S^2.}
\]

This is the abstract relational sphere.

The standard projective identification is

\[
\boxed{S^2\cong\mathbb{CP}^1.}
\]

Since

\[
\mathbb{CP}^1
=
(\mathbb C^2\setminus\{0\})/\mathbb C^\times,
\]

each projective point is a complex ray of a two-state Hilbert carrier. A normalized representative is

\[
|\psi(\theta,\phi)\rangle
=
\cos\frac\theta2|N\rangle
+
e^{i\phi}\sin\frac\theta2|S\rangle.

Therefore the non-circular continuation is

\[
\boxed{

\{N,S\}

S^2

\mathbb{CP}^1

\text{two-state quantum representation}.

\]

The term *Bloch sphere* is reserved for the sphere after this projective/Hilbert identification.
