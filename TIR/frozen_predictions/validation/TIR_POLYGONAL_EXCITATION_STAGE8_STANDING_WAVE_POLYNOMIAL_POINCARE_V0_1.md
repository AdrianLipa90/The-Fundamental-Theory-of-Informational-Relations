## 2. Finite nodal sets admit exact polynomial encoding

Given any finite set of distinct nodes

\[

\[

This step is algebraically exact and does not depend on a physical interpretation.

## 3. Dirichlet standing-wave sector → Chebyshev zeros

For a finite interval with Dirichlet endpoints, the spatial modes may be written

\[

\]

\[

\[
U_N(\cos\theta)=0.
\]

\[

\[

### Consequence

Standing-wave nodes on a line or finite interval naturally generate polynomial zero sets, but these roots are distributed on a real interval. They are not, by themselves, the vertices of a regular polygon in the complex plane.
