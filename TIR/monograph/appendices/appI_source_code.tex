\paragraph{Established context.}
The source-code appendix is governed by reproducible-research and scientific
computing practices \cite{Sandve2013,Wilson2014,Munafo2017,Stark2018}.  Executable
agreement with a formula establishes technical reproducibility, not empirical
truth of the underlying physical model.
% CITATION_CONTEXT_V10_9_END

The repository contains several executable layers with different evidential
roles.  They must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated ``model PASS''.

The 7 August 2026 review adds
\begin{center}
\path{TIR/validation/kappa_phase_rate_identity_v11_1.py}.
\end{center}
Its primary certificate is symbolic at the factor-exponent level.  The TIR
normalization is represented as
\[
\frac1{24}(\ln2)\pi^{-1},
\]
while angular-frequency conversion is represented as
\[
2\pi f.
\]
Exact multiplication gives
\[
\boxed{
\frac1{24}(\ln2)\pi^{-1}\times 2\pi f

so the rational coefficient $1/12$ and cancellation of the powers of $\pi$ are
certified without a floating-point approximation to either $\pi$ or $\ln2$.
Representative numerical frequencies are then evaluated only as secondary
implementation sanity checks.

\section{Interpretation of PASS and FAIL}

A successful execution may establish one of several distinct statements:
\begin{description}
\item[Technical PASS:] the declared implementation reproduces its frozen formula,
receipt or source contract.
\item[Formal PASS:] an exact or conditional algebraic statement is implemented
consistently with its assumptions.
\item[Physical PASS:] a prospective observable satisfies its frozen empirical
decision rule.
\item[Physical FAIL:] a frozen observable violates its empirical constraint even
when the corresponding computation is technically correct.
\end{description}

The current publication explicitly retains physical failures such as the active
neutron-EDM constraint.  A technical PASS must therefore never be substituted for
a physical PASS.
