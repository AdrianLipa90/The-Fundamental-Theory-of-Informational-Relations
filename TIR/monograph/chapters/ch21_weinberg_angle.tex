\section{Structural relation}

The current TIR weak-angle relation is

\begin{equation}
\sin^2\theta_W^{(0)}
= \frac{L_4}{L_3+L_4}+\kappa
= \frac29+\kappa.
\label{eq:weinberg}
\end{equation}

The first term $L_4/(L_3+L_4)=2/9$ is the tetrahedral edge ratio shared with
the corresponding mixing geometry, and $\kappa$ supplies the information-phase
correction.

Numerically,

\[

\]

The associated structural sine and cosine are

\[
\sin\theta_W^{(0)}=\sqrt{\sin^2\theta_W^{(0)}},
\qquad
\cos\theta_W^{(0)}=\sqrt{1-\sin^2\theta_W^{(0)}}.
\]

These quantities feed the frozen tree-level gauge-boson map in
Chapter~\ref{ch:gauge_bosons}.

comparison is requested.  The active TIR comparison therefore uses the typed

\[
\boxed{
\sin^2\theta_W^{(0)}
\xrightarrow{\;\mathcal R_W(\mu,\text{scheme})\;}

\]

The structural value may be compared numerically at coarse level, while a

across on-shell, effective-angle and running definitions.
