\chapter{Gauge Boson Masses}
\label{ch:gauge_bosons}

\section{Frozen candidate coupling}
The previous assignment is retained for audit:
\[
g_{\rm TIR}=\frac{L_4}{L_3}+\frac{L_4}{L_5}
           =\frac{24}{35}\approx0.68571.
\]
With the Metatime VEV this gives
\[
M_W^{\rm TIR}=83.96\ {\rm GeV},
\qquad
M_Z^{\rm TIR}=95.77\ {\rm GeV}.
\]

\section{PDG 2026 precision status}
These values are roughly $4.5\%$ and $5.0\%$ above the current reference
masses. Because $M_W$ and especially $M_Z$ are precision observables, the
discrepancies correspond to very large uncertainty-level pulls. The current
formulae are therefore classified as precision failures, not merely small
percentage offsets.

\section{No post-hoc repair}
The earlier illustrative replacement
\[
g\rightarrow g(1-\kappa)
\]
is not promoted as a validation repair. An observable-specific multiplicative
correction chosen after seeing $M_W$ or $M_Z$ would not provide independent
evidence.

A future electroweak construction must instead derive:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the renormalization scheme and scale of the couplings;
\item their running;
\item the relevant radiative corrections;
\item the relation between the frozen geometric quantities and measured
      pole/line-shape observables.
\end{enumerate}
Only after those ingredients are frozen may the masses be retested.
