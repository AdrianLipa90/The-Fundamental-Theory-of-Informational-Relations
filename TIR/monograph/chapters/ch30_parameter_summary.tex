\chapter{Validation-Aware Parameter Summary}
\label{ch:parameter_summary}

\section{Why a single mean percentage is not used}
The earlier global mean absolute percentage error is withdrawn as a validation
metric. The observables in this monograph have experimental uncertainties that
span many orders of magnitude; averaging relative percentage residuals hides
that distinction and also mixes prospectively testable outputs with
retrospectively revised or incomplete constructions.
