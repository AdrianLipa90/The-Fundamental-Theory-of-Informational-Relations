\section{Summary of Tensions}

The Metatime framework, while successful across most SM sectors, has
several unresolved tensions.

\subsection{CKM CP Phase (Resolved)}

The previous inversion of the Jarlskog relation gave
\(\delta = 73.6^\circ\) (\(\sigma = 5.35\)).  The direct geometric
formula:

\[
\delta_{\text{CKM}} = \arccos\frac{L_4}{L_5}
                    = \arccos\frac{2}{5}
                    = 66.42^\circ
\]

gives \(\sigma = 0.55\) relative to the PDG value
\(65.6^\circ \pm 1.5^\circ\).  The CP phase is now the dihedral angle
between the annihilation and intention axes of the NOEMA tetrahedron.

\subsection{Baryon Octet Systematic Offset (Resolved)}

The previous octet coefficients \(\beta = -177.91\)~MeV and
\(\gamma = 55.20\)~MeV gave a \(2.8\%\) systematic offset for \(p/n\)
and \(\Sigma\).  The refined coefficients

\[
\beta = -\alpha\,\frac{L_3^2-1}{L_3^2} = -185.90\ \mev,\qquad
\gamma = \alpha\,\frac{L_4(L_3-L_4)}{L_3^2} = 38.73\ \mev
\]

reduce the mean octet error from \(1.72\%\) to \(0.33\%\).  The
residual \(+1.10\%\) in the nucleon mass reflects the intrinsic
limitation of the Gell-Mann--Okubo formula, not a Metatime-specific
issue.

\subsection{Gauge Boson Masses (\(+4.5\%\))}

The SU(2) coupling \(g = L_4/L_3 + L_4/L_5 = 24/35\) gives \(M_W\) and
\(M_Z\) predictions \(4.5\%\) and \(5.0\%\) above the observed values.
The offset is systematic and may be resolved by a \(\kappa\) correction
or a full renormalization-group analysis.

\subsection{Higgs Mass (Resolved)}

The Higgs mass was the framework's most significant failure
(\(+4900\%\)).  The revised formula:

\[
M_H = v\,\kappa\,(L_3^2 + L_4 + L_5)
\]

gives \(M_H = 126.07\)~GeV, within \(0.66\%\) of the PDG value
\(125.25\)~GeV.  This sector is now one of the best-predicted.

\subsection{Neutron EDM (Factor 3 Above Bound)}

The predicted nEDM \(5.33 \times 10^{-26}\ e\cdot\text{cm}\) (using
exponent \(D = L_3+L_4+L_5 = 14\)) is a factor \(\sim\)3 above the
current experimental limit \(d_n < 1.8 \times 10^{-26}\ e\cdot\text{cm}\).
This is a genuine tension: the framework lacks a cancellation mechanism
at this level.  Next-generation experiments (n2EDM, PanEDM, SNS nEDM)
will reach \(10^{-27}\) to \(10^{-28}\ e\cdot\text{cm}\) within 3--5
years, testing whether this tension is resolved by a geometric
suppression or whether it constitutes a full falsification.

\subsection{Anomaly \(A_5\) (Baryon Number)}

The non-cancellation of \(A_5 = 1/6\) is expected (it's the SM baryon
anomaly), but its numerical coincidence with \(q_s/(L_3 L_4 N_c)\) is
noted without derivation.

\subsection{Collatz Quarter-Power Mass Bridge (Comparative Signal; Open)}

The accelerated odd Collatz map gives the asymptotic geometric-mean
multiplier

\[
\rho_C
=\exp\left(\ln3-\mathbb{E}[\nu_2(3n+1)]\ln2\right)
=\frac34,
\]

because uniform odd residue classes satisfy
\(\mathbb{E}[\nu_2(3n+1)]=2\).  Appendix~\ref{app:collatz_quarter_power}
therefore tests the predeclared bridge

\[
X_i=\frac{A_e}{A_i},
\qquad
\ln\frac{m_i}{m_e}
=
\frac{X_i^{3/4}-1}{L_3\kappa}
+
\frac{R_i-R_e}{\kappa},
\]

where \(A_i\) is the mass-free Euler--Berry action coordinate and
\(R_i\) is the mandatory Ramanujan release coordinate.

The \(3/4\) variant beats the fixed \(2/3\), \(1\), and \(4/3\)
comparators on mean, median, and maximum absolute logarithmic error and
preserves the generation ordering in all three charged-fermion families.
The isolated quarter-power trace nevertheless has a geometric-mean
multiplicative error of \(9.967\), so it is not a closed mass derivation.

\subsection{Sector-Holonomy Release (Retrospective Signal; Absolute Offset Open)}

Appendix~\ref{app:sector_holonomy_release} records the candidate

\[
\ln\frac{m_i}{m_e}=Q_i+G_i+H_i+W_i,
\]

with

\[
G_i=(L_5-c_i)\frac{\ell_i-\ell_e}{L_3},
\]

\[
H_i=
\frac{s_i}{\max(1,c_i)}
\ln\left(
\frac{1+|v_{7,i}|/\kappa}
     {1+|v_{7,a(i)}|/\kappa}
\right),
\]

and, for down quarks,

\[
W_i=L_4\ln\max_{u_j}
\left|\mathcal O_{\mathrm{open}}(u_j,d_i)\right|.
\]

The executable operator contains no observed non-electron mass, no observed
mixing element, no fitted continuous coefficient, and no particle-specific
residual correction.  However, its functional form was selected after review
of the v10.1 residual pattern.  It is therefore a retrospective structural
candidate rather than a prospective sealed validation.  The v10.2r1 ledger
supersedes the original v10.2 methodological label while preserving the
numerical trace.

As a retrospective diagnostic, the global non-anchor geometric-mean
multiplicative error falls from \(9.967\) to \(1.672\).  Excluding the
still-unresolved absolute \(u\)-sector baseline, the remaining seven
non-anchor slots have

\[
\overline{|\Delta\ln m|}=0.1320,
\qquad
\max|\Delta\ln m|=0.2540,
\qquad
\exp\overline{|\Delta\ln m|}=1.141.
\]

The generation ordering remains correct in every charged-fermion family.
The dominant residual is

\[
\frac{m_u^{\mathrm{pred}}}{m_u^{\mathrm{val}}}=24.18.
\]

This isolates an inter-sector baseline problem, but it does not provide an
independent confirmation.  A particle-specific subtraction is forbidden;
Debt~9 and canonical promotion remain open.

\section{Future Work}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Renormalization group flow}: Derive the running of gauge
couplings from the Poincar\'{e} disk curvature to fix \(g\) and the
gauge boson masses.
\item \textbf{Higgs sector}: Identify the correct structural formula for
\(M_H\), either as an exponential Kuramoto mass or as a modified VEV
relation.
\item \textbf{Kuramoto octet refinement}: Replace the static GMO
coefficients with a dynamical eigenvalue equation for \(\beta\) and
\(\gamma\).
\item \textbf{CKM \(|V_{cb}|\)}: Revise the \(|V_{cb}|\) formula to
incorporate a higher-order \(\kappa\) correction.
\item \textbf{Preregistered absolute up-sector baseline}: Before selecting
another formula, freeze the allowed colour--Berry--twin-prime inputs, the
common up-sector symmetry constraints, and an independent validation
observable.  The already inspected charged-fermion mass table is not a clean
prospective holdout.  No \(u\)-specific coefficient and no residual scan are
permitted, and the relative \(c/t\) release must remain unchanged.
\item \textbf{Anomalous magnetic moments}: Extend the framework to
\((g-2)_\mu\) and \((g-2)_e\).
\item \textbf{Dark matter}: Identify a dark matter candidate from the
Metatime particle spectrum (sterile neutrino? hidden-sector gauge boson?).
\item \textbf{Inflation}: Connect the metatime framework to the CMB
power spectrum via the Poincar\'{e} disk metric.
\end{enumerate}
