\section{Current physical status}
\begin{description}
  \item[Gauge bosons -- OPEN TENSION.] The frozen relations overshoot the quoted
  $W$ and $Z$ masses by approximately $4.5\%$ and $5.0\%$.  No
  renormalization-group derivation is presently supplied.

  \item[Higgs mass -- RETROSPECTIVE REVISION.] The earlier $6.3$~TeV formula
  failed.  The replacement $M_H=v\kappa(L_3^2+L_4+L_5)$ was adopted after the
  target value was known.  Its small percentage offset is not prospective
  evidence, and the statement ``within $2\sigma$'' has been withdrawn.

  \item[Neutron EDM -- PHYSICAL FAIL.] Correct arithmetic gives
  $\theta_{\rm QCD}=2.2208\times10^{-10}$ and
  $d_n=5.3299\times10^{-26}\,e\,\mathrm{cm}$, a factor $2.96$ above the bound
  used in the manuscript.  The former value $1.82\times10^{-26}$ is superseded.

  \item[Collatz quarter-power bridge -- OPEN.] The $3/4$ exponent beats the
  declared fixed comparators but leaves a geometric-mean multiplicative error
  $9.967$ and is not a closed charged-fermion spectrum.

  \item[Common up-sector baseline -- RESTRICTED NO-GO.] A common additive shift
  cannot repair the frozen v10.2 up-sector residuals; the unavoidable worst
  multiplicative error is at least $4.7207104$.

  \item[Separable candidate family -- PROSPECTIVE.] Exactly three candidates and
  the orthogonal ratios $y_c/y_\mu$ and $y_c/y_t$ remain frozen for the first
  qualifying direct post-29-July-2026 likelihoods.  No fourth candidate, refit,
  or observable substitution is allowed.
\end{description}

physical derivation debt.  The canonical PhaseNav dependency layer is bound to

\texttt{54f65f2ca7d35cdd98f0ab8984cc1a8d74444a96}, while the NOEMA
provenance contract is bound to commit
\texttt{42a0a8916e81ca27f2213bf0f28538f046c2e89a}.
These commit identities are provenance anchors, not empirical evidence.

\begin{description}

  The structural roles of the four generator slots are explicitly represented

  The project runtime contains an executable directed-orbit convention.  This
  is a project rule, not an externally established physical law.

  \item[Relational-gradient orientation -- IMPLEMENTED SOURCE OPERATOR / CANDIDATE TIR BINDING.]
  The source operator exists and is executable, but its use as the physical

  \item[TIR coefficient-slot binding -- OPEN.]
  Promotion requires a prospective map from coefficient-free atomic/orbital

Consequently the previous coarse statement that the whole role/orientation
problem was open is refined: the operator vocabulary and routing structure are
implemented, while the physical binding theorem remains open.  A PhaseNav
vector, holonomy receipt, NOEMA memory record, or successful software regression
cannot by itself promote an open, retrospective, or failed scientific claim.
In particular, the Collatz stopping-orientation pattern remains a retrospective
candidate requiring prospective validation, and the physical statuses listed
above are unchanged.
