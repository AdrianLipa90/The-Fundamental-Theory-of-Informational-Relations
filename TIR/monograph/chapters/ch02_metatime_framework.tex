\section{The Information Constant \(\kappa\)}

The Metatime framework defines the information constant
\begin{equation}
\kappa \equiv \frac{\ln 2}{24\pi}.
\label{eq:kappa_framework_definition}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Geometric-phase reference scale}

Consider a two-level quantum system whose Hamiltonian depends adiabatically on
parameters $\mathbf{R}(t)$. After a closed cycle the state may acquire a
geometric phase~\cite{berry1984}:
\begin{equation}
\gamma_n = i \oint \langle n(\mathbf{R}) | \nabla_{\mathbf{R}} n(\mathbf{R}) \rangle \cdot d\mathbf{R}.
\label{eq:berry_general}
\end{equation}
For a spin-$1/2$ eigenstate on the Bloch sphere,
\begin{equation}
\gamma = -\frac{\Omega}{2}\pmod{2\pi},
\label{eq:berry_spin_half}
\end{equation}
where $\Omega$ is the enclosed solid angle. A loop enclosing a hemisphere
($|\Omega|=2\pi$) gives a phase of magnitude $\pi$ modulo $2\pi$. A full-sphere solid angle $4\pi$ instead gives magnitude $2\pi$.
The factor $\pi$ used in the Metatime normalization is therefore a declared
spinorial/geometric reference phase scale.

\subsection{Binary Shannon information}

For a binary equiprobable distribution, Shannon entropy is~\cite{shannon1948}
\begin{equation}
H\!\left(\frac12,\frac12\right)=\ln2
\quad\text{(nats)}.
\label{eq:shannon_binary}
\end{equation}
This exact information-theoretic identity supplies the numerator of the TIR
normalization.

\subsection{Normalized recurrence before radians}

A cyclic process can first be represented by a normalized turn coordinate
\begin{equation}
q\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z,
\end{equation}
so that one recurrence is $q\mapsto q+1$. Its winding rate is $f_q=\Delta
N/\Delta t$. An angular coordinate is introduced by
\begin{equation}
\phi=Cq,
\end{equation}
where $C$ is the closure period; in radians $C=2\pi$.

For the two-level state used in the information-spinor subprogramme, the
balanced equatorial loop carries Berry holonomy $-1$. The dedicated
information-spinor analysis under \path{TIR/zeta_information_axis/} and
Appendix~\ref{app:information_spinor_crosswalk} keep this path-dependent
holonomy explicit.

\subsection{Tetrahedral normalization and discrete cross-factorization}

The rotational symmetry group of a regular tetrahedron is $A_4$ and has order
12. The full tetrahedral symmetry group including reflections is isomorphic to
$S_4$ and has order 24. The declared normalization uses
\begin{equation}
24 = 2\,|A_4|,
\qquad |A_4|=12.
\label{eq:tetrahedral_normalization}
\end{equation}
The exact arithmetic identity
\begin{equation}
24=8\cdot3=12\cdot2=6\cdot4
\label{eq:twentyfour_crossfactor}
\end{equation}
is also retained as an internal cross-factorization ledger. Its factors are
typed separately as declared sector/flavour, projective-cycle, and spinor-cycle
assignments. The $SU(3)$ Weyl group $S_3$ remains a separate mathematical
object.

\subsection{Structural normalization and cycle reconstruction of \(\kappa\)}

The TIR definition is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\kappa \equiv \frac{\ln 2}{2|A_4|\pi}
=\frac{\ln 2}{24\pi}}.
\label{eq:kappa_construction}
\end{equation}
Numerically,
\[
\kappa = 0.009193150006360484\ldots .
\]
Equivalently, if the declared information cycle assigns
\begin{equation}
\frac{\dd I}{\dd q}=\frac{\ln2}{12},
\label{eq:info-per-projective-turn}
\end{equation}
then conversion to the angular coordinate gives
\begin{equation}
\frac{\dd I}{\dd\phi}=\frac{\ln2}{12C},
\end{equation}
and for $C=2\pi$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\dd I}{\dd\phi}=\frac{\ln2}{24\pi}=\kappa.
\end{equation}
This representation separates the arithmetic cycle assignment from the angular
closure convention and exposes both in the validation contract.

\section{The Exponential Mass Formula}

A central Metatime ansatz for selected mass relations is
\begin{equation}
m = E_P \, e^{-S/\kappa}.
\label{eq:mass_formula}
\end{equation}
Here $E_P = \sqrt{\hbar c^5 / G} = 1.22089 \times 10^{22}$~MeV is the Planck
energy, and $S$ is a dimensionless action constructed from declared
L-constants, arithmetic assignments and sector-specific rules. The model is
audited sector by sector under the repository claim hierarchy.

\claimstatus{Model ansatz; physical validity determined sector by sector.}

\subsection{Why the Exponential Form?}

The exponential architecture is motivated by comparison with statistical
weights such as
\[
Z = \sum_i e^{-E_i / k_B T}.
\]
In the Metatime construction, $\kappa$ sets a dimensionless information-phase
scale and $S$ is used as an action-like coordinate. Agreement or failure of the
resulting mass relations is tracked empirically in the validation ledgers.

\section{Action Construction Principles}

The current model builds sector actions $S$ from combinations of:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Spin-labelled terms:} terms involving $1/2$ in fermionic sector assignments.
\item \textbf{Generation terms:} terms proportional to $\kappa$ in declared generation-release constructions.
\item \textbf{Flavour terms:} terms involving quark-prime assignments and L-constants $L_3,L_4,L_5$.
\item \textbf{Geometric corrections:} ratios or powers of declared geometric quantities and $\kappa$ used by specific candidate relations.
\end{enumerate}
Their evidential status is tracked separately for retrospective assignments and
prospective tests.

\section{The NOEMA Tetrahedron}

The NOEMA tetrahedron is a model-space construction in the operator coordinates
\[
\{\hat I,\ \hat M,\ \hat A\},
\]
where $\hat I$, $\hat M$, and $\hat A$ are the isospin-, mass-, and axial-labelled
coordinates used by the framework. TIR uses the tetrahedral picture to organize
candidate relations among flavour and mixing quantities. Their validation is
reported in the CKM and PMNS sectors.
