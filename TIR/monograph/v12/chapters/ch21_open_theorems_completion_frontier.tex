\chapter{Open Theorems and Completion Frontier}
\label{ch:v12_completion_frontier}

\paragraph{v12.1 synchronization.}
The original v12 frontier snapshot treated Cartan refinement and the
zero-torsion spatial sector as future gates. They are no longer open in that
form. The current repository has conditional/local theorem closure through
Gates A2--A4 and an implemented Gate-A5 global-manifold certifier. The
coefficient branch has also been narrowed: arithmetic evaluation of the
already-declared magnitude-parent packets is exact, while transition-level
parent selection remains open. The remaining frontier is therefore stated in
terms of production inputs, parent selection, cross-framework global admission,
dynamical normalization and unresolved physical bindings.

\section{Frontier as a dependency graph}

A TIR gate can be promoted only after every named parent surface is available
and the gate itself has a theorem, executable contract or evidence receipt
appropriate to the claim. The current v12.1 DAG audit is
\path{TIR/validation/tir_v12_1_completion_frontier_dag_v0_3.py}.
It intentionally avoids a frozen headline count of ``open problems'': the
status is attached to named nodes so later theorem closures cannot leave a
numerically correct but semantically stale summary.
