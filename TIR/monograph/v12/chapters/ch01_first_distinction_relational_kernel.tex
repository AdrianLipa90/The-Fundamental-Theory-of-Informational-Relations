\section{Dependency before temporal order}

The monograph begins with structural dependency. Write
\begin{equation}
\boxed{X\prec_{\rm dep}Y}
\end{equation}
when the admitted structure represented by $Y$ uses $X$ as an upstream parent in the derivation graph. Temporal ordering receives its own dynamical treatment in the companion time branch; the primitive order here is therefore dependency rather than chronology.

\section{Zero non-logical axioms}

The current TIR foundation declares
\begin{equation}
\boxed{N_{\rm nonlogical\ axioms}=0.}
\label{eq:v12_zero_axiom_count}
\end{equation}
Ordinary logic, definitions, typing rules and standard mathematical theorems used downstream remain background formal machinery. They are not counted as physical or ontological TIR axioms.

Literal ontic nothing is not introduced as an object. If a symbol or term denotes a member of the admitted ontic domain $\mathcal O$, it is already a referent and therefore not literal absence. TIR therefore types
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathsf{Nothing}\notin\mathcal O,\qquad 0\neq\mathsf{Nothing}.}
\label{eq:v12_nothing_not_zero}
\end{equation}
The arithmetic or coordinate value $0$ is retained only as a typed null value of a relation, map, residual or difference.

For a relational comparison $\Delta_R$,
\begin{equation}
\Delta_R(a,a)=0
\end{equation}
is relational nullity, not an ontic zero-object.

\section{Primitive exchange orbit}

A nontrivial relation has two orientation roles. Define orientation reversal
\begin{equation}
J:(a,b;R)\mapsto(b,a;R^{-1}),
\qquad
J^2=\mathrm{id}.
\end{equation}
For a non-self-collapsed distinction the two orientation classes are distinct. Rename them
\begin{equation}
\boxed{N=[a\to b],\qquad S=[b\to a].}
\label{eq:v12_primitive_binary_orbit}
\end{equation}
The exchange orbit is
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\mathcal O_J=\{N,S\}.}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Primitive binary orbit]
For a nontrivial relational dyad with orientation reversal $J$, the orbit under $\langle J\rangle$ contains exactly the two orientation roles $N$ and $S$.
\end{theorem}

The pole pair is therefore not inserted as an independent axiom; it is the two-role closure of the primitive relation.

w_N+w_S=1.

\begin{equation}

\label{eq:v12_half_fixed_point}
