# The Space of Geometry: From First Distinction to Pythagoras

Paper outline v0.1

## Abstract target

Construct the shortest auditable derivation chain from the primitive TIR distinction structure to a local Euclidean spatial carrier and terminate the derivation at Pythagorean closure. The central finite geometric object is the regular tetrahedron, appearing as the minimal full-dimensional simplex in the three-dimensional relational generator carrier. An independent qubit informational-completeness route supplies a cross-check through the tetrahedral SIC structure.

State explicitly that the information-theoretic and spatial tetrahedra are separately typed until the promotion theorem identifies them.

## 8. Pythagorean closure

For orthogonal `A,B`,

\[
\langle A,B\rangle=0,
\]

so

\[
\|A+B\|^2
=\|A\|^2+\|B\|^2.
\]

Set

\[
a=\|A\|,\quad b=\|B\|,\quad c=\|A+B\|
\]

and obtain

\[
\boxed{a^2+b^2=c^2.}
\]

This is the closure point of the paper.
