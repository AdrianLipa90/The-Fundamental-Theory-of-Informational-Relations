## 2. Spatial realization criterion

Let \(V_x\) be a finite-dimensional real local direction carrier. Require:

1. continuous orthogonal action of the derived \(SO(3)\);
2. faithfulness, so admitted relational rotations remain distinguishable;
3. minimality among nonzero real carriers satisfying 1--2.

## 3. Minimal faithful dimension

No faithful continuous orthogonal \(SO(3)\) representation exists in real dimension one or two. The defining representation in real dimension three is faithful and orthogonal. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb R}V_x=3.}

\[
\boxed{
V_x\cong\mathbb R^3\cong\operatorname{Herm}_0(2).
}
\]
