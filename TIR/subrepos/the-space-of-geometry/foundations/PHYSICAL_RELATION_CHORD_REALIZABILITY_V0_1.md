## 1. Physical binary quantum-point domain

A physical normalized two-level state has Bloch form

\[
\rho(\mathbf r)=\frac12\left(I+\mathbf r\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\right),
\qquad |\mathbf r|\le1.
\]

The affine hull is

\[
\mathcal A_2=\frac12I+\operatorname{Herm}_0(2),
\]

but the physical state set is the Bloch ball

\[
\mathbb B^3=\{\mathbf r\in\mathbb R^3:|\mathbf r|\le1\}.
\]

For two physical endpoints define the generator-normalized relation

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal E_{xy}=2(\rho_y-\rho_x)
=(\mathbf r_y-\mathbf r_x)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma.
}
\]

Let

\[
\mathbf d_{xy}:=\mathbf r_y-\mathbf r_x.
\]

## 2. Exact reachable-chord theorem

For physical endpoints,

\[
|\mathbf d_{xy}|
\le |\mathbf r_y|+|\mathbf r_x|
\le2.
\]

Hence every physical single-edge relation lies in the closed radius-two ball of the translation carrier.

Conversely, let any

\[
\mathbf d\in\mathbb R^3,
\qquad |\mathbf d|\le2
\]

be given. Choose

\[
\mathbf r_x=-\frac12\mathbf d,
\qquad
\mathbf r_y=+\frac12\mathbf d.
\]

Then

\[
|\mathbf r_x|=|\mathbf r_y|=\frac12|\mathbf d|\le1,
\]

so both endpoints are physical density states and

\[
\mathbf r_y-\mathbf r_x=\mathbf d.
\]

Therefore the reachable coefficient-vector set is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal D_{\rm phys}
=\mathbb B^3_{2}
:=\{\mathbf d\in\mathbb R^3:|\mathbf d|\le2\}.
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\rm phys}

\{\mathbf d\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma:\ |\mathbf d|\le2\}

This subset contains an open neighborhood of the zero relation and spans the full three-real-dimensional carrier.

## 4. Exact physical right-triangle family

Let \(\mathbf u,\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^3\) satisfy

\[
|\mathbf u|=|\mathbf v|=1,
\qquad
\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v=0.
\]

Choose real numbers

\[
a\ge0,
\qquad
b\ge0,
\qquad
a^2+b^2\le1.
\]

Define three physical Bloch vectors

\[
\mathbf r_x=0,
\qquad
\mathbf r_y=a\mathbf u,
\qquad
\mathbf r_z=a\mathbf u+b\mathbf v.
\]

Their norms satisfy

\[
|\mathbf r_x|=0,
\qquad
|\mathbf r_y|=a\le1,
\qquad
|\mathbf r_z|^2=a^2+b^2\le1,
\]

so all three endpoints are physical density operators.

The consecutive relation vectors are

\[
\mathcal E_{xy}=a\,\mathbf u\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
\]

\[
\mathcal E_{yz}=b\,\mathbf v\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
\]

and endpoint composition gives

\[
\mathcal E_{xz}
=(a\mathbf u+b\mathbf v)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma.
\]

Because

\[
\langle\mathcal E_{xy},\mathcal E_{yz}\rangle
=ab\,\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v
=0,
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal E_{xz}\|^2
=\|\mathcal E_{xy}\|^2
+\|\mathcal E_{yz}\|^2
=a^2+b^2.
}
\]

Hence the Pythagorean identity is not only a carrier identity: it is realized by an explicit nonempty family of triples of physical binary quantum-point states.
