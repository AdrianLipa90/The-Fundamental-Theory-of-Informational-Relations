## 2. Carrier versus physical chord domain

The affine translation carrier is the full vector space

\[
V=\operatorname{Herm}_0(2)\cong\mathbb R^3.
\]

The subset generated by one ordered pair of physical density operators is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal R_{\rm phys}
=\{\mathbf d\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma:\ |\mathbf d|\le2\}.
}
\]

The forward bound follows from

\[
|\mathbf r_y-\mathbf r_x|\le2.
\]

The converse is exact: for any \(|\mathbf d|\le2\),

\[
\mathbf r_x=-\frac12\mathbf d,
\qquad
\mathbf r_y=+\frac12\mathbf d
\]

are physical endpoints and realize the prescribed relation.

Thus the physical chord set is bounded inside one local state fiber while containing a neighborhood of zero and spanning all three real carrier directions.
